Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your StableBus account and associated personal data without signing in to the app.

Submit a deletion request

Enter the mobile number registered with StableBus. Your email application will open with a pre-filled request addressed to our support team.

Include your country code, for example +91.

Your email app will open. You must send the email to submit your request.

What happens next?

1. Your email app opens with your deletion request pre-filled.

2. Send the email to StableBus.

3. We may contact you to verify ownership before processing the request.

4. After verification, we process deletion using our account-deletion process. Some records may be retained where legally required.

Important: Opening your email app does not submit the request. You must send the email.
