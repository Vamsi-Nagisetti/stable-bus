Privacy Policy

This policy explains how StableBus handles information when you use our mobile application or website.

This Privacy Policy applies to the StableBus mobile application and the StableBus website, including the pre-registration form. It describes the information we collect, how we use it, and when it may be shared to provide our services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use StableBus, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: your mobile number and any name or email address you provide when creating or using your account.
  • Pre-registration information: name, mobile number, email address and city submitted through our website's pre-registration form.
  • Passenger and booking information: passenger details you provide, travel dates, routes, selected bus and seats, boarding and dropping points, ticket details, and booking or cancellation status.
  • Payment-related information: transaction reference, payment amount and payment status needed to confirm and manage bookings. Payment credentials are handled through the applicable payment service provider; StableBus does not ask you to share your payment PIN or password with us.
  • Support and communication information: details you provide when contacting us about your account, booking or experience.
  • Technical information: limited information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the app, such as app version, device model and operating-system version, where made available to us.
StableBus does not request your device's GPS location for the bus-booking service. We do not currently use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Device identifiers and permissions, if introduced or required by a future app version or service component, will be described in an updated policy.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your mobile number using OTP.
  • To search for buses and provide seat selection, booking and ticketing services.
  • To process and verify payments and provide booking-related updates.
  • To retrieve booking, cancellation and refund information and help manage related requests.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your questions.
  • To administer pre-registration and send relevant StableBus launch information.
  • To protect the service, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. How information is shared

We share information only as needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations. Depending on the feature you use, recipients may include:

  • Bus operators and ticketing/API service providers: passenger and journey information needed to check availability, make or manage a booking, issue a ticket or process a cancellation.
  • Payment service providers: payment-related information needed to process or verify a transaction or refund.
  • OTP and communication providers: your mobile number and delivery information needed to send verification codes or service messages.
  • Infrastructure and technology providers: information needed to host, secure and operate StableBus.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities: where disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.

We do not sell your personal information. Service providers receive only the information needed for the services they perform for StableBus.

4. Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain booking and transaction records, support customers, resolve disputes, prevent misuse and comply with legal requirements. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete it or de-identify it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

5. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your StableBus account through the account-deletion option available within the StableBus app. We may ask you to verify your account before processing the request. Some booking, payment or transaction records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate accounting, dispute-resolution or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to provide information requested by the app. However, certain features, such as OTP login or ticket booking, may not be available without the information needed to provide them. You can use the account-deletion option in the app to request deletion of your account, subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. Children's privacy

StableBus is a general bus-booking service and is not designed specifically for children. Where a booking includes a child as a passenger, the booking should be made by a parent, guardian or other person authorised to provide the passenger information.

9. Third-party services and links

StableBus may rely on third-party services to provide features such as bus inventory, ticketing, payments, OTP delivery and hosting. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the applicable policies of those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page. Please review it periodically.

11. Contact StableBus

For questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact StableBus through the support/contact option provided in the app or on our website.
